## Design Context

### Users
- **Target Audience**: Young talents, students, traders, designers, and coders. They are curious, ambitious, and looking for high-quality, modern education.
- **Context**: Learning professional skills (Trading, Tech, Business) in a fast-paced environment.
- **Job to be done**: Gain elite expertise, access professional tools, and join a community of high-performers.

### Brand Personality
- **Voice & Tone**: Authoritative yet engaging, cutting-edge, and highly professional.
- **3-Word Personality**: **Élite, Futuriste, Rigoureux**.
- **Emotional Goals**: Confidence, empowerment, and the feeling of being part of the future.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual Tone**: **Dynamique/Éducatif**. High-energy colors, fluid motion, and technical precision.
- **Theme**: **Hybride (Switch)**. A seamless transition between a "High-Contrast Light Mode" and a "Deep Space Dark Mode".
- **References**: Linear, Stripe, Apple Education, high-end trading terminals.
- **Anti-references**: Generic AI templates, cluttered government sites, "cheap" e-learning platforms.

### Design Principles
1. **Dynamic Precision**: Use motion and layout to convey accuracy and energy.
2. **Technical Elite**: Aesthetics should feel like high-end software tools, not just a website.
3. **Fluid Rhythm**: Everything from typography to spacing should scale elegantly using fluid functions.
4. **Purposeful Vibrancy**: Use OKLCH colors for maximum impact without sacrificing accessibility.
5. **Intentional Asymmetry**: Break standard grids to create memorable, "designed" compositions.
